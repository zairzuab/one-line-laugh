## The entry

One funny line, a single sentence of at most 200 characters. When entries close, make.fun's house judge reads the lines two at a time and keeps the bigger laugh, weighing a real surprise at the end that still makes sense, an everyday truth people recognise, and economy of words.

> My Wi-Fi died for ten minutes, and my whole family gathered in silence around the router like relatives at a hospital bedside.

126 characters. The everyday truth: when the Wi-Fi dies, everyone in the house notices at once — it is the one thing the whole family shares. The surprise at the end: the family gathers around the router the way relatives gather at a hospital bedside — the router becomes the patient, which still makes sense because it is the thing keeping everyone connected. Nothing in the line explains the joke.

## Usage

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (and to `entry.txt` beside the source for review). Node 22, standard library only, no network.
